# Answers (evidence: transcript.txt + 9 frames only)

1. Early punches did not hurt: they were angry blows, and anger produces "spiritual pressure"/torment energy, which is the only thing the Grand Priest regenerates from (he does not regenerate from ordinary strikes), so the blows fed him [23:00]-[23:36] (Whis explaining). Goku's change: he dropped anger, struck calmly with eyes closed, "pure light", no impurities or spiritual pressure, so the strikes gave the Priest no fuel [22:19]-[23:52], [19:50]-[20:15] (Goku: light only responds to calm/purity). Seen: frame am00_243s shows a gold fist hitting a teal-clad body with sparks (a punch landing; the identity of the target is my inference). The reason is from the transcript only.

2. The "royal creation" is Ozerius, the ancient first king and Zeno's father, whose soul/identity the Priest sealed and turned into an incomplete shell [31:25]-[32:50]. He hands Vegeta "my soul, my essence, my blade" to carry his legacy [33:31]; Goku frames it as "giving you his soul" [33:57]. Afterwards Vegeta appears in a "sacred form" [34:39]. (Transcript only; the frames do not show the handover.)

3. Secret: the two guardians are one mind/one soul in two bodies [53:30]. Each body anticipates and cancels attacks [53:47]-[54:15]; they merge when hit to fake a full hit and split under heavy pressure; pain is shared between the bodies and damage heals very fast [54:21]-[54:50]. To beat them: land perfectly synchronized strikes on both bodies with equal power and no timing deviation [54:59] (Piccolo); i.e. two identical attacks at the same moment, same purity, double power, which per Goku requires Goku and Vegeta to become one (fuse) [56:19]-[56:34].

4. First fusion failed because, per Whis, Goku's body was rebuilt entirely through the necklace so his biology differs from Vegeta's, and the mixed energy found no equilibrium [59:05]-[59:18]. The visitors (Goten and Trunks, then Zeno's father Ozarius and mother, named in the transcript as "Alicia") said a real fusion needs purified intention: no rivalry, ego, pride or anger, emptying the heart of conflict, trust and surrender to each other; light and will must complete each other; "when your feelings become one, the essence will move on its own" [60:36]-[62:26]. They then calm down and fuse [62:36]-[64:41].

5. Called Gojita [67:03], [67:18]. With "one percent" he raised both hands and released a pure compressed air blast, not a strike, energy wave or technique [68:39]-[69:03]. It fragmented/blew apart the guardians' bodies, leaving them gasping with energy at rock bottom but not destroyed (he says they can rebuild from one core and he meant only to weaken them) [69:10]-[70:02]. (They are later wiped out by an unrelated black lightning bolt [70:47]-[71:01].) Frame am08_4131s (68:51) shows a smiling face with a gold swirl-engraved helmet, gold face markings, gold hoop earring and glowing eyes; I infer it is Gojita.
