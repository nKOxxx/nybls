## Q1: V
Answer: Sheikh Abdulaziz al-Tarifi (الشيخ عبد العزيز الطريفي); held in Irbid, Jordan (مدينة إربد، الأردن), in Sha'ban 1434 AH (شعبان ١٤٣٤ هـ).
Evidence: g_00000s.png title card: "محاضرة 'تعظيم الله عز وجل' للشيخ عبد العزيز الطريفي" and "عقدت في الأردن، مدينة إربد، في شعبان ١٤٣٤ هـ". Transcript has none of these (it opens at [00:02] with a greeting and [00:20] "فالله سبحانه وتعالى").
Key terms: عبد العزيز الطريفي / Abdulaziz al-Tarifi; إربد / Irbid; الأردن / Jordan; شعبان ١٤٣٤ / Sha'ban 1434.
Partial: speaker name only, or Jordan without the city, or year without month (or vice versa).

## Q2: S
Answer: He says "Woe to you! Do you know who Allah is?" (twice), then glorifies Allah repeatedly (subhan Allah) until the change shows in his face. He then says Allah is above His Throne, the Throne is above His heavens, and the heavens are like this, with his hand spread like a dome (qubba).
Evidence: transcript [07:37]-[08:50]; esp. [08:18] "ويحك أتدري الله", [08:25] "يسبح", [08:39]-[08:50] "الله فوق عرشه ... على هيئة قبة". 
Partial: rebuke plus glorification but no Throne/dome, or only the Throne statement.

## Q3: S
Answer: 500 years of travel between the earth and the lowest heaven; 500 years between each heaven and the next; each heaven's thickness is 500 years; and 500 years between the lowest and highest part of the Throne.
Evidence: transcript [09:30] "ما بين الأرض وبين السماء الدنيا مسيرة خمسمائة عام", [09:54]-[10:03] between heavens and each heaven, [10:03] "ما بين أدنى العرش وأعلاه مسيرة خمسمائة عام".
Partial: states 500 years but only for earth-to-heaven.

## Q4: S
Answer (any of these counts, full answer is most): (1) tawhid: worship, fear, hope and love none but Allah ([15:14]-[15:49]); (2) not being conceited about one's worship, since Allah is greater ([15:52]-[16:27]); (3) freedom from servitude to others and to desire ([18:35]-[18:40]); (4) belittling what one spends or suffers for Allah, and trials ([18:44]-[20:18]); (5) worshipping Allah equally in secret and in public, e.g. night prayer alone ([30:54]-[32:17]); (6) avoiding sins even when alone ([31:30]-[31:47]); (7) not relapsing from the truth under trial or when people leave ([32:34]-[34:12], Ibrahim "was a nation").
Partial: three or fewer correct items.

## Q5: S
Answer: People retaliate in proportion to the stature of the opponent: a 3-year-old child insulting you, a mosquito bite or an ant sting does not provoke revenge ([27:47]-[29:11]). Allah is not harmed by anyone (hadith qudsi "you will never reach My harm" [29:32]), the world is not worth a mosquito's wing to Him [29:46], so the lack of immediate punishment is a respite (imhal) [29:57], and thinking He must avenge himself comes from likening Allah to humans ([30:43]-[30:51]).
Evidence: transcript [27:22]-[30:51].
Partial: gives child/mosquito analogy only, or respite only.

## Q6: S
Answer: No. A person must worship one of three: Allah, something other than Allah, or his own desire. He cites "أفرأيت من اتخذ إلهه هواه" (Have you seen the one who took his desire as his god, Al-Jathiyah 45:23).
Evidence: transcript [17:49]-[17:57] "اختر إلهك: إما الله، وإما غير الله، وإما هواك"; [18:09]-[18:11] verse. Surah number is not stated in speech.
Partial: three options without the verse, or verse without options.

## Q7: B
Answer: Title "Ta'zeem Allah Azza wa Jalla" (تعظيم الله عز وجل, "Glorifying/Revering Allah"). Root cause of shirk: ignorance of Allah's greatness, so people assign that greatness to others.
Evidence: title from g_00000s.png (V part; banner "مسابقة تعظيم" in g_00019s.png). Cause from transcript [06:15]-[06:23] "ما من شرك ... إلا هو بسبب الجهل بعظمة الله"; [07:05] "السبب أنك جهلت الله فعظمت غير الله"; [06:41]-[06:57] fly parable.
Key terms (V part): تعظيم الله عز وجل.
Partial: cause without the title, or title without cause.

## Q8: S
Answer: Cursing/insulting Allah is worse than idol worship and even worse than atheism. Idolaters honoured Allah and made idols as an offshoot of that reverence; the atheist denies a creator exists; the one who curses affirms the Creator yet attacks Him, which is obstinacy and defiance. "Not meaning it" is not accepted, as it would not be for insulting one's father or a ruler.
Evidence: transcript [21:50]-[22:46], [23:03]-[23:24].
Partial: ranking correct without reasons.
