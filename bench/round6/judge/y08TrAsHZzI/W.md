3. Walking routine [03:26-04:16]. This assumes a wireless mic and that you move away from the podium:
   1. Walk away from the podium and don't touch it.
   2. Take two or three steps, then stop.
   3. Tell a story or make a point, and ask a question.
   4. Take two or three steps back, or move to another spot on the stage.
   5. Repeat a few times over the talk. Don't march along the stage the whole time.
   Mic care: don't bump the mic and don't trip over the cord [03:33-03:37]. The cord warning is inferred to apply to corded mics. He says it generally, then says "hopefully" you have a wireless one.
