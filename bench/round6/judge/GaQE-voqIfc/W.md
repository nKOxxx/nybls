# Answers

1. Early punches did not hurt because the Grand Priest does not regenerate from strikes in general but gains strength when exposed to anger/spiritual pressure. Whis [23:00-23:28]: the early blows "were filled with anger. Anger generates spiritual pressure, and that is precisely what fuels my father... He regenerates only when he is exposed to one specific type of energy. The energy of torment and spiritual pressure." Gohan: so the early strikes strengthened him [23:28]. (Transcript note: Whis says the Priest is "my father"; the transcript also shows the Priest saying at [14:55] that Goku's torture became fuel.) What Goku changed: his strikes became "pure light. No anger, no impurities, no spiritual pressure" [23:44]; he fought calmly with eyes closed [22:27]. Goku's own realization [19:50-20:15]: light "responds to purity, to calmness, to peace." Result: his energy no longer fed the Priest [23:52].

2. The "royal creation" is Ozerius, the ancient king and first ruler, who is Zeno's father [31:25, 32:43]. The Grand Priest sealed his memories and split his soul, body and identity, turning him into an incomplete shell [31:42, 32:05]. Before collapsing he tells Vegeta to carry his legacy: "Take it. My soul, my essence, my blade" [33:31]. Goku frames it as his father "giving you his soul" [33:57]. Vegeta then appears in a "sacred form" [34:39-34:47]. (Name spelled "Ozerius"/"Ozarius" in auto-captions; Vegeta is garbled as "Vajida"/"Vigita".)

3. Secret: the two guardians are one mind, one soul, two bodies [53:30]. One body anticipates the opponent's moves and cancels them; they merge into one body when attacked to look like a single target, split to process damage; pain is shared across both and heals rapidly [53:47-54:50]. To beat them (Piccolo, 54:59): land perfectly synchronized strikes on both bodies with equal power and no timing deviation. Goku adds [56:19]: "two identical attacks at the same moment with the same purity and with double the power. That will never happen unless we become one", i.e. fuse. Whis notes this is nearly impossible for two separate minds [55:13-55:37].

4. Failure: stated by Whis [59:00-59:18]: fusion needs total balance between two bodies, but Goku's body was rebuilt entirely through the necklace, so his biology differs from Vegeta's; the energies found no equilibrium and the fusion collapsed. The visitors (Zeno's father Ozarius and mother Alicia, with Goten and Trunks) said it needs purified intention [61:47-62:19]: no speck of rivalry, ego, or desire to best the other; empty the heart of conflict, trust and surrender your will to one another; "when your feelings become one, the essence will move on its own." Goten/Trunks: "You are halves. You do not compete. You complete each other" [60:28]. Light (Goku) and Will (Vegeta) are incomplete without each other [61:08-61:31]. Inference: the transcript gives two explanations (biology per Whis, rivalry/ego per the visitors); it does not say which one is "the" cause.

5. Called Gojita (the transcript's spelling of Gogeta) [67:03]. At [68:39] he says "I'll only use one percent of my power," raised both hands, and released "not a strike, not an energy wave... just pure compressed force. A pressurized air blast" from his palms [68:55-69:10]. Result: the blast passed through the guardians, bodies fragmenting and dissolving in a flash; they were left gasping, energy at rock bottom, barely upright [69:10-69:36]. He said it was enough to weaken them, not eliminate them, since they can rebuild using one core [69:43-69:57]. Afterward (not by him) a black lightning bolt from the sky destroyed them and they "disappeared from existence entirely" [70:39-71:01].
