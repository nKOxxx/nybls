# Ground truth (video GaQE-voqIfc, ~73 min Dragon Ball narration; grid every 37 s)

Note: author could view grid frames from g_02035s onward only (earlier frames were not rendered to author due to a request limit); all visual claims below use frames from 2035s onward.

## Q1 — S
Answer: Whis explains the Grand Priest regenerates only when exposed to one type of energy, the energy of torment and spiritual pressure generated by anger. Goku's early blows were angry, so they fueled the Priest. Goku switched to strikes of pure light (no anger, no impurities, no spiritual pressure), calm, eyes closed, so they give no fuel.
Evidence: transcript 23:00-23:52 ("An anger generates spiritual pressure ... He regenerates only when he is exposed to one specific type of energy. The energy of torment and spiritual pressure"; "His strikes have become pure light. No anger, no impurities, no spiritual pressure"). Also 19:50-20:15 (Goku: light responds to purity, calmness, not rage) and 22:19-22:34 (eyes closed, calm).
Partial: says anger/rage fueled the Priest, or that pure/calm light stopped helping, but not both parts.

## Q3 — S
Answer: The two guardians are one mind/one soul in two bodies. One body anticipates the opponent's moves and cancels them; they merge into one body when attacked so the attacker thinks he hit the whole target, then split when pressure is critical; damage to one is split across both and heals fast. To beat them, Goku and Vegeta must land perfectly synchronized strikes on both bodies with equal power and no timing deviation (Goku concludes they need two identical attacks at the same moment, same purity, double power, i.e. become one).
Evidence: transcript 53:30-54:50 ("we are one. One mind, one soul, two bodies"; merging/splitting, pain split, heals), 54:59-55:13 (Piccolo: perfectly synchronized strikes on both bodies with equal power), 56:19-56:34 (two identical attacks at the same moment ... unless we become one).
Partial: one-mind-two-bodies stated without the synchronized-strike requirement, or vice versa.
