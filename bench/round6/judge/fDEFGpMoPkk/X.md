# Answers

1. Static and current. Static = studies of electric charge at rest (stationary); current = electric charge in motion [01:55-02:18]. He says "electricity" itself simply means electric charge [00:48-00:58]. Board 01:30 frame: Electricity with arrows to "Static" and "Current".

2. Symbol capital Q (board "Electric charge (Q)", frame 01:30; spoken [01:08]). SI unit coulomb. Abbreviation: capital C, written "[C]" on the board (frame 04:55; spoken abbreviation is garbled in transcript [01:30]). Named after Charles Augustin Coulomb [01:34].

3. "Energy associated with a moving charge" [02:56, 03:18]; board reads "Current Electricity: Energy associated with a moving charge." (frame 04:55).

4. H.L = higher level, L.L = lower level (water in cylinder A at higher level, B at lower) [07:30, 07:35]; S = the stop(cock) in the side tube connecting the cylinders [07:02]. The dotted (red) line = the common water level once flow stops, i.e. both levels equipotential [08:28-08:36]. (Board frame 09:00 shows H.L, L.L, S, dotted lines.)

5. A is the wider cylinder and is on the left (B narrower, on the right). Evidence: frames 09:00 and 13:05 (A label under wide left cylinder). Spoken: one broader, one narrow [06:47-06:55].

6. Starting temperatures 80 deg C (hot) and 20 deg C (cold) [09:24, 09:30; board "80C", "20C"]. Surroundings isolated/insulated, no heat entering or leaving the system [09:35-09:50]. Final state: thermal equilibrium [10:41]. Final common temperature: spoken "10 degrees celsius" [10:35] (note: this is inconsistent, being below 20; transcript may be mis-heard but is as transcribed, UNVERIFIED by audio); written on board as "T deg C" in a circle (frame 11:05, 13:05), i.e. not a number.

7. Protons: because electrons had not been discovered yet, early physicists (Ampere et al.) took flow of positive charge/protons as the current [05:10-05:43, 14:16-14:30]. Board (frame 04:55): proton = p with superscript 1 and subscript +1 (i.e. 1 over +1, "1 +1 p" stacked at left of p); electron = e with superscript 0 and subscript -1.

8. 1.5 volt printed on the cell [12:29-12:32]. Positive end is treated as high potential (higher level), negative as low potential [12:39-12:55]. Drawing (frame 13:05): rectangle labelled "1.5 V" with "+" on the left end (a small knob nub) marked "H.L" with a tick, and "-" on the right end marked "L.L" with a tick.
