- **Why the second layer was necessary:** anticipating the bust, the streamer pre-built a **second wall-off behind the first** (depots plus a flown-in barracks, "I could actually have the second wall off ready" [08:09–08:11], "I have the second layer of wall ready" [08:37]); he then re-walled the bunker position [09:19–09:24]. His verdict: "The second layer of wall was completely necessary ... every single reinforcement that he would have sent would have not been able to get through ... This barracks actually helped so much" [11:13–11:30]. (He also notes a nydus would have beaten him outright [10:51–11:12].)
