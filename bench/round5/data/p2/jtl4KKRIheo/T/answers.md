## 2. Battery before starting

**Answer (from transcript):** Discharge the battery to below 25% before beginning. It matters because a charged lithium-ion battery can catch fire and/or explode if accidentally damaged during the repair. Also power the phone off.

**Evidence:** [00:39]-[00:46] "before you begin your repair discharge your battery to below 25 ... a charged lithium-ion battery can catch fire and or explode if you accidentally damage it"; [00:48] "power off your phone". The "%" is inferred (ASR dropped the unit); 25% is the standard iFixit threshold (own knowledge).

## 5. Cracked screen, suction won't grip

**Answer (from transcript):** Cover the display with a piece of clear packing tape so the suction cups can stick. The video also notes that using an iSclack makes the step easy; if you only have a single iFixit suction handle, follow the opening procedure on the ifixit.com guide. (Heating with the iOpener for about a minute to soften the adhesive is given as the general step before this, not specifically a cracked-screen workaround.)

**Evidence:** [01:02]-[01:10] "if you have a cracked display suction cups might have a hard time attaching to the glass if you're having trouble getting them to stick cover the display with a piece of clear packing tape"; [01:11]-[01:17] "if you have an ice clack on hand the next step will be easy but if you only have an ifixit suction handle follow the opening procedure on the guide at ifixit.com". The transcript gives no other workaround (e.g. no mention of a heavy-duty cup or glue).

## 6. Opening pick order and clipped edge

**Answer (from transcript):** 
1. Insert the pick into the gap at the bottom edge (created by the iSclack).
2. Slide it around the lower-left corner and up the left edge.
3. Re-insert at the bottom edge and slide up the right side.
4. Top edge last: this edge is held by both adhesive and clips. Differently there: gently pull the right side of the display down slightly toward the Lightning port, insert the pick in the top-right corner, and while gently pulling down slide the pick across the top edge, cutting the adhesive.
Then open the display by swinging it up from the left side like a book's back cover, without lifting it fully off (ribbon cables).

**Evidence:** [01:39]-[01:48] insert pick in gap, "slide the opening pick around the lower left corner and up the left edge"; [01:59]-[02:06] "insert it again at the bottom edge ... slide it up the right side"; [02:06]-[02:20] "the top edge of the display is held on with both adhesive and clips so gently pull the right side of the display down slightly towards the lightning port and insert your pick in the top right corner while gently pulling down slide the pick across the top edge"; [02:25]-[02:34] swing up from the left side.

## 7. Earpiece speaker / front sensor assembly transfer

**Answer (from transcript):** Four screws: three Phillips and one Y000. Reinstall order on the new display: (1) press the proximity sensor and flood illuminator into their slots; (2) slide the ambient light sensor back into place with tweezers and place its bracket straight onto it; (3) press the microphone back into place with a finger; (4) press the flex cable below the microphone onto the display so it is well adhered (spudger optional); (5) flip the speaker assembly over and reinstall the four screws.

**Evidence:** [03:27]-[03:34] "remove the front assembly begin by removing four screws three being phillips and one y triple zero"; [04:36]-[05:06] reassembly sequence: proximity sensor/flood illuminator [04:40]-[04:44], ambient light sensor and bracket [04:46]-[04:49], microphone [04:52]-[04:54], flex cable [04:56]-[05:03], flip speaker assembly and four screws [05:03]-[05:06].
