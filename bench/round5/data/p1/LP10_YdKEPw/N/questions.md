(timestamps and/or frame filenames). If you cannot determine an answer from your
evidence, say so explicitly rather than guessing — a confident wrong answer
scores worse than an honest "insufficient evidence".

   media handle shown on screen.

   it say, and roughly when does it appear?

   gloves worn throughout the whole video, or only during part of it?

4. How are the disassembled parts cleaned? Describe the method(s) shown.
