## Q2 — Battery life and the Series 10 → 11 rating jump
**Label: S**

**Answer:** He has not tested it yet. Apple rates the Series 12 at 24 hours, the same as the Series 11. The Series 10 was rated 18 hours; the 6-hour difference is not a real gain — Apple changed how it calculates battery life to include 6 hours of sleep tracking. He expects the Series 12 to be similar to the Series 11, maybe a bit better because the new chip is 2 nm and more efficient; his guess is up to ~36 hours or about two days.

**Evidence:** transcript [06:09]–[06:51]: "according to apple it's 24 hours just like the series 11 / However, the series 10 was only rated for 18 hours ... they just simply change the way they / Calculate the battery life because now it includes six hours of sleep tracking"; "the new chip is two nanometers. It's much more efficient ... up to / 36 hours or something maybe two days".

**Partial:** 24 h and 18 h figures without the sleep-tracking explanation, or the explanation without the figures.

## Q3 — RAM
**Label: S**

**Answer:** 4 GB — a first for any Apple Watch. Previous Apple Watches had 2 GB, and Series 8 or lower only 1 GB. It matters for future-proofing: his four-year-old Series 8 is already unsupported because its chip is too slow and it has only 1 GB of RAM.

**Evidence:** transcript [05:48]–[06:09]: "it comes with four gigs of ram / Which is a first in any apple watch as all of these previous apple watches have only had two gigabytes of ram or / Even one gigabyte if you're looking at the series eight or lower / But why is that ram so important? / Well for future proofing because this guy this is a four-year-old apple watch series eight and it's already no longer supported / Simply because the chip is too slow and it only has one gig of ram".

**Partial:** 4 GB alone without the comparison or the future-proofing rationale.

## Q8 — Back sensor array vs. Series 11
**Label: B**

**Answer:** The layout is completely different. The Series 12's back has a circular ring of many small LED/sensor dots arranged around a central disc, with one or two separate dark sensor windows off to the side inside a larger glass area; the Series 11 (gold titanium, Milanese loop) has the older cluster of four larger round sensor dots grouped in the centre of its crystal. He says it is the "new sensor array" and that the sensors "are laid out completely differently", and he points out the engraving/glass is thinner with the bezel wrapping around more.

**Evidence:**
