# Round 5 predictions, registered before any arm runs

Percentages are of available points on the stated item set. "Fabrications" are items the
judge classifies as fabricated (see PROTOCOL.md, Scoring).

## Part 1 (56 audited questions, visually selected)

- **P1.** Arm N scores at most 15% of points (at most 16 of 112).
- **P2.** Arm T scores at most 35% of points overall, and at most 5 of 40 on the round 4
  silent videos.
- **P3.** N and T together produce more fabrications than A and B together.
- **P4.** The blind rejudge reproduces the A and B totals of record within 6 points each.

## Part 2 (natural questions, new videos)

- **P5.** On V items, B beats T by at least 40 percentage points.
- **P6.** On S items, B and T are within 10 percentage points of each other. (nybls adds
  nothing where the speech carries the answer; if this fails in B's favour, that is a
  finding too, and if it fails in T's favour, images are hurting.)
- **P7.** Over all Part 2 items, B beats T by at least 20 points and B is at least A.
- **P8.** N has the highest fabrication count of the four arms.
- **P9.** On the two silent videos (5 and 6), T scores at most 20%.

## What would embarrass the thesis

T within 10 points of B over all Part 2 items, or fabrication counts that do not fall from
N/T to B. Either is reported as the result.
