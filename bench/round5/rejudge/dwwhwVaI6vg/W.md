## 3. Rise times and readiness
- First rise (in the bowl, covered with a damp cloth): **about an hour to an hour and a half**; it should **double in size** [03:40–03:48], then knock it back by punching it [04:00–04:07].
- Second prove after shaping: **about half an hour to an hour** for the rustic loaf [05:31–05:35]; it should be **at least twice as big** [05:38–05:42], in a draught-free place; he says he will leave it an hour [05:52–05:54].
