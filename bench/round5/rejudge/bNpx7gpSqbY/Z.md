# Answers


1. **Speaker/organization:** Bill Gross. He founded Idealab, the incubator whose company data he analyzed; he says Idealab has started more than 100 companies over 20 years.

2. **Five factors:** the idea, the team/execution, the business model, the funding, and the timing.

3. **Attribution and rank order:** 1st timing 42%, 2nd team/execution 32%, 3rd idea 28%, 4th business model 24%, 5th funding 14%. The analysis covered about 200 companies: roughly 100 Idealab companies and 100 non-Idealab companies (Airbnb, Instagram, Uber, YouTube, LinkedIn, and others). No timestamp available.

4. **Named companies:** Successes: Citysearch, CarsDirect, GoTo (later Overture), NetZero, Tickets.com. Failures: Z.com, Insider Pages, MyLife, Desktop Factory, Peoplelink. I am fairly confident of these lists but cannot cite evidence; treat as own-knowledge recall, not verified.

5. **Z.com scorecard, timing and funding:** insufficient evidence. I recall he rates Z.com highly on idea, team, business model and funding and low on timing, but I do not reliably remember the exact numbers out of 10 and will not guess.

6. **Why Z.com failed:** it was an online entertainment company around 1999-2000 when broadband penetration was too low and watching video online was hard (codec/plug-in problems), so it went out of business in 2003. When YouTube launched in 2005, two things had changed: the codec problem had been solved by Adobe Flash, and broadband penetration had crossed 50 percent in the US. No timestamp available.

7. **Assessing timing:** look honestly at whether consumers are really ready for what you have to offer, and be really honest about it, not in denial about the results you see, because when you love something you want to push it forward regardless. No timestamp available.

8. **Airbnb scores on the five factors:** insufficient evidence. I recall he scores Airbnb highly across the board and highlights that its timing (people needing extra income during the recession) was key, but I do not reliably remember the individual out-of-10 numbers and will not guess.
